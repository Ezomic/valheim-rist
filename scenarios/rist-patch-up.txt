# Rist: Swift-mending's capstone, Patch-up. Ten seconds after your last swing or hit taken, a quarter
# of the health you lost comes back over five seconds.
#
# `rist hurt <n>` takes a plain hit through the game's own ApplyDamage, which is a hit taken as far as
# the capstone can tell, and `rist show` prints
#
#   patch-up: not carved | carved, lost L, mending M, mended N times (H health)
#
# L is what has fallen off the health bar since the last mend and is owed a quarter of, M is what is
# still being paid out, N and H count mends and the health they gave back.
#
# FOOD WRECKS THIS TEST. Food regen heals a little every ten seconds, and the capstone counts every
# rise in health that is not its own as already recovered, so a fed character reads a smaller "lost"
# than was dealt. Run it with no food eaten. Health below full is also needed to be healed at all,
# and the character needs more than 10 health: a character with no food has 25.
#
# THE BOUNDARY MATTERS MOST: rank four must owe nothing. A capstone paid before it is earned passes
# any test that only ever looks at rank five.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client. About 25 seconds.
#
# It leaves Swift-mending at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank swiftmend 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed patch-up: not carved yes

# ---- rank 4: hurt, wait, and nothing is owed or paid.

mod rist rank swiftmend 4
wait 1
mod rist hurt 8
wait 1
mod rist show
printed patch-up: not carved yes

# ---- rank 5: the loss is counted, and ten quiet seconds start the mend.

mod rist rank swiftmend 5
wait 12
mod rist show
printed patch-up: carved, lost 0.0 yes

mod rist hurt 8
wait 1
mod rist show
printed lost 8.0 yes

# Ten seconds after the hit the mend begins and the tally is spent: a quarter of 8 is 2.
wait 10
mod rist show
printed lost 0.0 yes

# And five seconds later it has been paid in full.
wait 6
mod rist show
printed mended 1 times (2.0 health) yes

mod rist rank swiftmend 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed patch-up: not carved yes
