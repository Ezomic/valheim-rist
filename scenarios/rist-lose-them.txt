# Rist: Soft step's capstone, Lose them. A creature that has lost you gives up the hunt after 12 s
# instead of 30.
#
# Three stones put a flag on the player's ZDO for creature and ship owners to read (Soft step, Quiet
# wake and Weatherly), and `rist show` prints what a client would read off this character:
#
#   carried: lose them yes|no, sleepers sleep on yes|no, rides the waves yes|no
#
# That is the half a scenario can assert, and it is the half that decides whether the capstone works
# on a creature another player's machine owns: the flag is read off the ZDO, not off this process.
# The give-up itself is a hunt that has to be broken off, so it is read in play: be seen by a
# Greydwarf, break line of sight and stay out of earshot, and count. It stops hunting after about
# 12 seconds, not 30. A boss and a creature that hunts players keep going.
#
# THE BOUNDARY MATTERS MOST: rank four must publish nothing.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Soft step at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank softstep 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed carried: lose them no yes

mod rist rank softstep 4
wait 2
mod rist show
printed carried: lose them no yes

mod rist rank softstep 5
wait 2
mod rist show
printed carried: lose them yes yes

mod rist rank softstep 0
wait 2
mod rist show
printed carried: lose them no yes
