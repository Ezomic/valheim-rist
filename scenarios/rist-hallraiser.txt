# Rist: Hall-raiser, a crafting station covers 20% more ground a rank for building, and at rank five
# the hammer places, removes and repairs from twice as far.
#
# It is the REACH version. The stamina discount was dropped: a discount on a discount was too thin.
#
# `rist show` asks the game, never the hand:
#
#   station reach: NAME B m, reaches E m (xR)   B is the station's own radius. E is found by asking
#                                               CraftingStation.HaveBuildStationInRange at growing
#                                               distances until it stops answering, so R is the
#                                               reach the build menu and PlacePiece actually get.
#   no-spawn circle xC                          the station's effect-area collider, the circle that
#                                               keeps enemies from spawning in a base, against the
#                                               station's own radius. Nothing writes it, so it has
#                                               to read x1.00 at every rank. That is the line that
#                                               catches a widened m_buildRange, which would widen
#                                               the circle with it.
#   hammer reach xH (A m of V m, TABLE)         Player.m_maxPlaceDistance against what it was, and
#                                               the piece table in hand. H moves only while the
#                                               hammer's table is.
#
# The boundary is read first: at rank 4 the station reach is x1.80 and the hammer is still x1.00,
# because the arm is the capstone. The hoe is then put in hand at rank 5 to prove the arm is the
# hammer's alone and Jafna's hoe is not doubled on the way.
#
# Not covered, because a scenario has no second player: that two players at one bench each get
# their own answer. That is by construction (the point asked about is the local player's), and
# worth one look with two clients.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Hall-raiser at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank hallraiser 0
place piece_workbench 4 0
give Hammer:1
give Hoe:1
wait 3

mod rist show
printed reaches yes

# ---- rank 0: vanilla.

mod rist show
printed (x1.00) yes

mod rist show
printed no-spawn circle x1.00 yes

mod rist show
printed hammer reach x1.00 yes

# ---- rank 4: the boundary. Four ranks of station reach and no arm.

mod rist rank hallraiser 4
tool Hammer
wait 3

mod rist show
printed (x1.80) yes

mod rist show
printed no-spawn circle x1.00 yes

mod rist show
printed hammer reach x1.00 yes

# ---- rank 5: double the reach, and the arm.

mod rist rank hallraiser 5
wait 3

mod rist show
printed (x2.00) yes

# The circle that keeps enemies out of a base stays where it was.
mod rist show
printed no-spawn circle x1.00 yes

mod rist show
printed hammer reach x2.00 yes

# ---- the arm is the hammer's alone.

tool Hoe
wait 2

mod rist show
printed hammer reach x1.00 yes

mod rist show
printed (x2.00) yes

mod rist rank hallraiser 0
wait 2

mod rist show
printed (x1.00) yes
