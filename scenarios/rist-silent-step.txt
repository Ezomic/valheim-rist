# Rist: Quiet wake's capstone, Silent step. Crouched and moving no faster than a crouch-walk, or standing, you
# make no noise at all.
#
# `rist crouch [off]` crouches or stands the character as the key does, and `rist noise` makes a noise of
# range 30 (a jump's) from a cleared noise slate, through the game's own AddNoise, and prints what the
# game recorded for it:
#
#   rist: noise asked 30.0, emitted X (crouching yes|no, speed S m/s)
#
# Standing, X is 30.0 less what the stone's ranks take off: 18.0 at rank five (-8% a rank, 40%), 20.4 at rank
# four. Crouched and still at rank five, X is 0.0: the noise was dropped before it was recorded. The
# crouch needs a second for the animation, and a crouching character is asserted from the printed line so
# a crouch that did not take cannot pass as silence.
#
# Not asserted: a crouch-walk at full speed (a scenario cannot hold the keys) and anything faster than a
# creep making noise again. Read both in play: creep with the key held, hit a tree and a creature close
# by hears nothing; stand up or sprint and it does.
#
# THE BOUNDARY MATTERS MOST: rank four must stay as loud as its ranks make it, crouched or not.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Quiet wake at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank quietwake 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed silent step: not carved yes

mod rist crouch off
wait 1
mod rist noise
printed emitted 30.0 yes

# ---- rank 4: crouched and still, still the ranks' noise and no capstone.

mod rist rank quietwake 4
wait 1
mod rist show
printed silent step: not carved yes
mod rist crouch on
wait 1.5
mod rist noise
printed crouching yes yes
printed emitted 0.0 no

# ---- rank 5: crouched and still, nothing; standing, the ranks' 18.0.

mod rist rank quietwake 5
wait 1
mod rist show
printed silent step: carved yes
mod rist noise
printed crouching yes yes
printed emitted 0.0 yes

mod rist crouch off
wait 1.5
mod rist noise
printed crouching no yes
printed emitted 18.0 yes

mod rist rank quietwake 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed silent step: not carved yes
