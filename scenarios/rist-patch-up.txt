# Rist: Swift-mending's capstone, Patch-up. Ten seconds after your last swing or hit taken, a quarter
# of the health you lost comes back over five seconds.
#
# `rist hurt <n>` takes a plain hit through the game's own ApplyDamage, which is a hit taken as far as
# the capstone can tell, and `rist show` prints
#
#   patch-up: not carved | carved, lost L, mending M, mended N times (H health)
#
# L is what has fallen off the health bar since the last mend and is owed a quarter of, M is what is
# still being paid out, N and H count mends and the health they gave back.
#
# Food regen heals a little between steps and a fading meal trims the maximum health, so a fed character
# reads a smaller "lost" than was dealt. The checks are therefore movements (the tally rises, is spent,
# one mend is paid), never exact figures. Health below full is needed to be healed at all.
#
# THE BOUNDARY MATTERS MOST: rank four must owe nothing. A capstone paid before it is earned passes
# any test that only ever looks at rank five.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client. About 35 seconds.
#
# It leaves Swift-mending at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank swiftmend 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed patch-up: not carved yes

# ---- rank 4: hurt, wait, and nothing is owed or paid.

mod rist rank swiftmend 4
wait 1
mod rist hurt 8
wait 1
mod rist show
printed patch-up: not carved yes

# ---- rank 5: the loss is counted, and ten quiet seconds start the mend.
#
# The character is fed and its health pool is large, so no figure is asserted to the decimal: food
# regen heals a little between steps and a fading meal trims the maximum, and a scenario that
# demands "lost 8.0" fails on a healthy capstone. What is asserted is the movement: the tally rises
# on a hit, is spent when the mend begins, and exactly one mend is paid.

mod rist rank swiftmend 5
mod rist hurt 1 full
wait 12
mod rist show
printed patch-up: carved yes
note mends mended\s(\d+)\stimes
note owed lost\s([\d.]+),
note back \(([\d.]+)\shealth\)
note mid mending\s([\d.]+),

mod rist hurt 70
wait 1
mod rist show
went owed lost\s([\d.]+), up

# Ten seconds after the hit the mend begins and the tally is spent into the mend. The hit is 70, not 8:
# food regen repays a small hit inside the quiet ten seconds and leaves nothing to mend.
# The read is about twelve seconds after the hit, so the five-second payout is still running.
wait 11
mod rist show
went owed lost\s([\d.]+), down
went mid mending\s([\d.]+), up
went mends mended\s(\d+)\stimes +1

# And five seconds later it has been paid out, and a quarter of what was lost is health returned.
wait 5
mod rist show
printed mending 0.0 yes
went mends mended\s(\d+)\stimes same
went back \(([\d.]+)\shealth\) up

mod rist rank swiftmend 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed patch-up: not carved yes
