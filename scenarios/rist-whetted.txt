# Rist: Sure hand's capstone, Whetted. Axes and pickaxes lose 40% less durability.
#
# A swing's wear cannot be staged: it comes off a tool that has struck something, and a scenario
# swings nothing. What is asserted is the boundary, that Whetted is the fifth rank's and not the
# fourth's and that the old +12% stamina regen is gone. The saving is read in play: carve Sure
# hand to five, chop a tree with an axe at a known durability, and compare. Ten swings cost six
# durability where they cost ten, and `rist show` keeps the total it put back.
#
# What `rist show` prints:
#
#   whetted: not carved | carved, 40% less wear on axes and picks, saved D durability here
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Sure hand at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank surehand 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed whetted: not carved yes

mod rist rank surehand 4
wait 1
mod rist show
printed whetted: not carved yes

mod rist rank surehand 5
wait 1
mod rist show
printed whetted: carved, 40% less wear on axes and picks yes

mod rist rank surehand 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed whetted: not carved yes
