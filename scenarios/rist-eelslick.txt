# Rist: Eel-slick, a dodge roll's invulnerable window 7% longer a rank, and at rank five a perfect
# roll makes the next roll free.
#
# `rist roll` queues a roll the way the key does, and `rist perfect` sends a hit into the roll in
# progress the way an attack does, which the game's own RPC_HitWhileDodging then counts as a perfect
# roll. Both need devcommands like the rest of `rist`, and both only make sense in singleplayer.
#
# What `rist show` prints, all measured and none of it Rist's opinion of itself:
#
#   roll window Vs, asked +A%, got Gs   V is how long the game's own window lasted, from the roll
#                                       starting to the DodgeMortal animation event. A is the share
#                                       the hand asked for, which is what is asserted. G is how long
#                                       the roll actually held, which the end of the roll's animation
#                                       can cut short, so it is read in the log and not asserted.
#   free roll armed Ns | not armed      the free roll waiting for the next roll.
#   last roll cost X stamina            stamina before and after the roll started, on the frame it
#                                       started. "(free)" when the free roll paid for it.
#
# THE ONE THING THIS CAN FIND OUT: whether +35% fits inside the roll. If the animation ends first,
# "got" comes out shorter than V * 1.35. That is the design question the ticket left open, and it is
# answered by reading the log line after rank 5, not by a failing step.
#
# A perfect roll needs an enemy within 8 metres, the same rule as Answering blow, so a Neck is
# spawned in front of the player. God mode keeps it from mattering.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Eel-slick at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank eelslick 0
wait 1

# ---- rank 0: the vanilla window, measured, and no free roll.

mod rist roll
wait 2

mod rist show
printed asked +0% yes

mod rist show
printed free roll not armed yes

# ---- rank 4: the boundary. A window 28% longer, and no capstone yet.

mod rist rank eelslick 4
wait 1

mod rist roll
wait 2

mod rist show
printed asked +28% yes

# A perfect roll at rank 4 must not arm anything, which is the capstone paid before it is earned.
spawn Neck
wait 1
mod rist roll
wait 0.25
mod rist perfect
wait 2

mod rist show
printed free roll not armed yes

banish Neck 30
wait 1

# ---- rank 5: 35% longer, and the capstone.

mod rist rank eelslick 5
wait 1

mod rist roll
wait 2

mod rist show
printed asked +35% yes

spawn Neck
wait 1
mod rist roll
wait 0.25
mod rist perfect
wait 0.5

mod rist show
printed free roll armed yes

# The roll that follows is paid for by the perfect one: nothing comes off the stamina bar.
wait 1.5
mod rist roll
wait 2

mod rist show
printed last roll cost 0.0 stamina (free) yes

# And it was spent, not kept: the next roll finds nothing armed.
mod rist show
printed free roll not armed yes

banish Neck 30
mod rist rank eelslick 0
wait 1
