# Rist: Keen hand's capstone, Combo holds. A weapon combo chains from a swing up to 0.6 s late.
#
# Vanilla drops a combo back to its first hit when the next swing starts more than 0.2 s after the
# last one ended. The capstone reads that gap 0.4 s shorter. The old capstone, +5% damage on every
# weapon, is gone, so this also reads that the damage did not come back.
#
# The boundary is what a scenario can assert; the chain itself is a timing a scenario cannot
# press. In play: equip a sword, swing, wait about half a second, swing again. Without the capstone
# the second swing is a first hit again. With it, it is the second hit of the combo, and `rist show`
# counts it.
#
# What `rist show` prints:
#
#   combo hold: not carved | carved, 0.6s to chain, held N chains
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Keen hand at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank keenhand 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed combo hold: not carved yes

mod rist rank keenhand 4
wait 1
mod rist show
printed combo hold: not carved yes

mod rist rank keenhand 5
wait 1
mod rist show
printed combo hold: carved, 0.6s to chain yes

mod rist rank keenhand 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed combo hold: not carved yes
