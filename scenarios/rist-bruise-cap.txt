# Rist: Thick-hided's capstone, Bruise cap. No single hit can take more than half of your maximum
# health.
#
# `rist hurt <n> [enemy] [full]` takes a hit through the game's own ApplyDamage and prints what came off
# the health bar and what share of the maximum that was: "hurt asked 1000.0, took 12.5 (50% of max
# health 25.0)". `full` tops the health up first, so every step starts from a full bar and the readings do
# not depend on the one before. With `enemy` the hit is typed as a creature's; without it the hit is typed
# Self, which the cap skips by design (a health cost is a price and not a blow), and that is the control.
#
# GOD MODE keeps a big hit from killing and floors the health at 1, so an uncapped big hit reads as almost
# the whole bar and not as the hit's size. That is what the rank 4 steps rely on: a 1000 hit with no cap
# takes the bar down to 1, which is 96% of a 25 point bar and is never 50%.
#
# A small hit is asked for as 2, which is under half of any maximum health a character can have, so it
# must land in full at every rank. The cap is half of the maximum whatever the maximum is, so the big
# hit is asserted as a share, not as a number: "(50% of max health".
#
# THE BOUNDARY MATTERS MOST: rank four must not cap. A capstone paid before it is earned passes any test
# that only ever looks at rank five.
#
# Not asserted, because an engine cannot reach them: damage over time and the fall. Burning and poison
# arrive through ApplyDamage typed Burning and Poisoned and are skipped by type; a fall is one hit typed
# Fall and is capped. Read both in play (see the changelog).
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Thick-hided at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank thickhide 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed bruise cap: not carved yes

# ---- no capstone: the big hit takes the bar down to the floor and the small one lands in full.

mod rist hurt 1000 enemy full
printed (50% of max health no

mod rist hurt 2 enemy full
printed took 2.0 yes

# ---- rank 4: still no capstone.

mod rist rank thickhide 4
wait 1
mod rist hurt 1000 enemy full
printed (50% of max health no

mod rist show
printed bruise cap: not carved yes

# ---- rank 5: the big creature hit is cut to half of the maximum, the small one is untouched, and a
# ---- hit typed Self (the control) is not capped.

mod rist rank thickhide 5
wait 1
mod rist show
printed bruise cap: carved, no hit takes more than 50% of max health yes

mod rist hurt 1000 enemy full
printed took yes
printed (50% of max health yes

mod rist hurt 2 enemy full
printed took 2.0 yes

mod rist hurt 1000 full
printed (50% of max health no

mod rist show
printed capped 1 hits yes

mod rist rank thickhide 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed bruise cap: not carved yes
