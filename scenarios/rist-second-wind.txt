# Rist: Long wind's capstone, Second wind. The first time your stamina runs out, a quarter of the bar
# comes back at once, and not again for 90 s.
#
# `rist spend <n>` spends stamina through Player.UseStamina, the call swinging and sprinting go
# through, so a big number runs the bar dry the way a long sprint does. `rist show` prints
#
#   second wind: carved, ready | ready in Ns, used N times, stamina P%
#
# P is the bar as a share of its maximum, read straight after the spend. The bar starts to refill
# one second after a spend, so the reads below come first and take well under it.
#
# THE BOUNDARY MATTERS MOST: rank four must read 0% after the bar is emptied, and a capstone paid
# before it is earned passes any test that only ever looks at rank five.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Long wind at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank longwind 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed second wind: not carved yes

# ---- rank 4: empty is empty.

mod rist rank longwind 4
wait 1
mod rist spend 9999
wait 0.2
mod rist show
printed stamina 0% yes

# ---- rank 5: the bar comes back by a quarter the first time it runs dry.

mod rist rank longwind 5
wait 6
mod rist spend 9999
wait 0.2
mod rist show
printed stamina 25% yes

mod rist show
printed used 1 times yes

# ---- and not again inside 90 s: emptied a second time it stays empty.

wait 3
mod rist spend 9999
wait 0.2
mod rist show
printed stamina 0% yes

mod rist rank longwind 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed second wind: not carved yes
