# Rist: Weatherly's capstone, Rides the waves. Slams into waves do no hull damage while you hold the
# helm, and collisions with rocks, ice and the shore cost the hull half as much.
#
# The same flag word as Lose them: `rist show` prints what a client would read off this character,
#
#   carried: lose them yes|no, sleepers sleep on yes|no, rides the waves yes|no
#
# and that flag is the half that decides whether the stone works on a ship another player's machine
# owns, since the owner reads the helmsman's flag off the ZDO. The slam itself needs a boat in rough
# water: in play, carve Weatherly to five, take the helm in a storm and let the bow fall onto a
# wave. The hull's health does not move. Take the helm off and it does.
#
# THE BOUNDARY MATTERS MOST: rank four must publish nothing.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Weatherly at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank weatherly 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed rides the waves no yes

mod rist rank weatherly 4
wait 2
mod rist show
printed rides the waves no yes

mod rist rank weatherly 5
wait 2
mod rist show
printed rides the waves yes yes

mod rist show
printed rides the waves: carved, hull slams do nothing while you hold the helm, collisions cost 50% of the damage yes

mod rist rank weatherly 0
wait 2
mod rist show
printed rides the waves no yes
