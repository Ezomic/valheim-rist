# Rist: Sure-footed's capstone, a dodge pressed as you land makes the fall count 8 m shorter.
#
# The old capstone was a second -50% on fall damage, rank five immune to falling. It is a landing
# roll now, and the ranks stay at -10% each. `rist fall <m> [roll]` raises the record of how high
# the character has been to <m> above the ground it stands on, so the next physics step is a real
# landing from that height through the game's own UpdateGroundContact, and with `roll` a dodge is
# pressed in the same call, which is inside the 0.3 s window. Nothing is climbed.
#
# What `rist show` prints, on the "falls:" line, all worked out at the landing:
#
#   last fall Fm, counted Cm, landing roll yes|no, fall damage D
#
# F is the height the game would have measured, C the height it was handed after the roll, and D
# the damage it will charge, worked out through SEMan.ModifyFallDamage, the call the game makes. God
# mode swallows the damage itself, which is why the number is read here and not off the health bar.
# Whole numbers, so a metre of jitter on the ground cannot move a line.
#
# Fall damage is (C - 4) / 16 of 100, less the stone's 10% a rank: 20 m is 100, 12 m is 50. So
# rank 0 reads 100 for 20 m, rank 4 reads 60, rank 5 reads 50, and rank 5 with the roll counts 12 m
# and reads 25. The old capstone's -100% would have read 0 at rank 5 with no roll at all.
#
# THE BOUNDARY IS THE POINT. The capstone must not leak into rank 4: a stone whose capstone quietly
# applied at every rank would still pass at rank 5. Rank 4 with a roll must count the whole fall.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER, same as the others: ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
# Needs stamina for the roll, and flat ground to stand on.
#
# It leaves Sure-footed at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank surefooted 0
wait 1

# ---- rank 0: the vanilla fall. 20 m is the whole bar.

mod rist fall 20
wait 0.5
mod rist show
printed last fall 20m, counted 20m, landing roll no, fall damage 100 yes

# ---- rank 4: -40%, and a roll pressed as you land does nothing yet.

mod rist rank surefooted 4
wait 1

mod rist fall 20 roll
wait 1
mod rist show
printed last fall 20m, counted 20m, landing roll no, fall damage 60 yes

wait 2

# ---- rank 5: -50%, and the capstone. No roll: the percentage alone, which is the old -100% gone.

mod rist rank surefooted 5
wait 1

mod rist fall 20
wait 1
mod rist show
printed last fall 20m, counted 20m, landing roll no, fall damage 50 yes

wait 2

# ---- rank 5 with the roll: 20 m counts as 12, which is half the bar, and half of that is 25.

mod rist fall 20 roll
wait 1
mod rist show
printed last fall 20m, counted 12m, landing roll yes, fall damage 25 yes

wait 2

# ---- a short fall is spent whole: 12 m less 8 is 4, which does not hurt at all.

mod rist fall 12 roll
wait 1
mod rist show
printed last fall 12m, counted 4m, landing roll yes, fall damage 0 yes

wait 2

# ---- the roll is spent by the landing: the next fall, with no new press, counts in full.

mod rist fall 20
wait 1
mod rist show
printed last fall 20m, counted 20m, landing roll no, fall damage 50 yes

mod rist rank surefooted 0
wait 1
