# Rist: Thick-hided's capstone, Shrug. A creature's hit worth under 4% of your maximum health does
# nothing.
#
# `rist hurt <n> [enemy]` takes a hit through the game's own ApplyDamage and prints what came off the
# health bar: "hurt asked 0.5, took 0.0". With `enemy` the hit is typed as a creature's, which is
# the type Shrug judges; without it the hit is typed Self and nothing judges it, which is the
# control, and the same number must come off in full.
#
# The line is 4% of maximum health, and a character with no food has 25, so the line is 1.0. 0.5 is
# under it at any health the character can have, and 5 is over it until maximum health passes 125.
# If the second pair fails on a well-fed character, that is the line having moved and not a bug:
# 5 is 4% of 125.
#
# THE BOUNDARY MATTERS MOST: rank four must not shrug. A capstone paid before it is earned passes any
# test that only ever looks at rank five.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client. The character should be
# at full health; god mode keeps the hits from killing, and takes nothing off them.
#
# It leaves Thick-hided at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank thickhide 0
wait 1

# ---- no capstone: a small creature hit lands in full.

mod rist hurt 0.5 enemy
printed took 0.5 yes

# ---- rank 4: still no capstone.

mod rist rank thickhide 4
wait 1
mod rist hurt 0.5 enemy
printed took 0.5 yes

mod rist show
printed shrug: not carved yes

# ---- rank 5: the small hit does nothing, the big one lands, and a small hit of another type lands.

mod rist rank thickhide 5
wait 1
mod rist show
printed shrug: carved yes

mod rist hurt 0.5 enemy
printed took 0.0 yes

mod rist hurt 5 enemy
printed took 5.0 yes

mod rist hurt 0.5
printed took 0.5 yes

mod rist show
printed shrugged 1 hits yes

mod rist rank thickhide 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed shrug: not carved yes
