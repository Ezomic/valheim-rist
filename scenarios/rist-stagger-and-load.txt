# Rist: Steady footing at -5% a rank, and Ox-backed's capstone off stagger and onto the load.
#
# Measured with `rist powers` on 2026-09-24: Fader's power is -0.50 stagger, and Steady footing's
# five ranks at -0.06 plus Ox-backed's old -0.15 capstone took the total to -0.95 - a hit filled
# the stagger bar at a twentieth of its rate. Steady footing is -0.05 a rank now, and Ox-backed
# finishes on walking overloaded costing half the stamina instead.
#
# `rist show` reads both off the game: stagger through SEMan.ModifyStagger with a base of 1,
# which is what every hit is scaled by, and the overloaded drain as a ratio of the Player's own,
# which is prefab data. The Ox-backed steps check stagger alongside, because the point of the
# change is that its capstone no longer touches stagger at all.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. It leaves both stones at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank steadyfoot 0
mod rist rank oxback 0
wait 1

mod rist show
printed stagger x1.00 yes

mod rist show
printed overloaded x1.00 yes

# ---- Steady footing: 5% a rank.

mod rist rank steadyfoot 5
wait 1
mod rist show
printed stagger x0.75 yes

# ---- Ox-backed: nothing at rank 4, half the overloaded drain at rank 5, and stagger untouched.

mod rist rank oxback 4
wait 1
mod rist show
printed overloaded x1.00 yes

mod rist rank oxback 5
wait 1
mod rist show
printed overloaded x0.50 yes

# The old capstone was -0.15 stagger. With Steady footing at 5 this would read x0.60 if it were
# still there.
mod rist show
printed stagger x0.75 yes

mod rist rank steadyfoot 0
mod rist rank oxback 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed stagger x1.00 yes

# Restored, not merely stopped: the drain is written onto the player and has to come back.
mod rist show
printed overloaded x1.00 yes
