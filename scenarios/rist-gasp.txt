# Rist: Tide-borne's capstone, Gasp. After your stamina runs out in water, drowning damage waits 6 s
# before its first tick instead of 1.
#
# A scenario cannot swim: it would need deep water under the character and an empty bar held for
# seven seconds, and it would run in god mode, where the ticks do not show. What is asserted here is
# the boundary, that Gasp is the fifth rank's and not the fourth's and that the old swim stamina
# capstone is gone. The wait is read in play: carve Tide-borne to five, swim out into deep water,
# run the bar dry and count. The first drowning tick lands about six seconds after the bar empties,
# where vanilla's lands after one, and `rist show` keeps the seconds it held back.
#
# What `rist show` prints:
#
#   gasp: not carved | carved, drowning waits 6s, held back S s here
#
# SINGLEPLAYER. Ranks live on the server and `rist rank` refuses on a client.
#
# It leaves Tide-borne at rank 0.

clear
god

mod rist rank tideborne 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed gasp: not carved yes

mod rist rank tideborne 4
wait 1
mod rist show
printed gasp: not carved yes

mod rist rank tideborne 5
wait 1
mod rist show
printed gasp: carved, drowning waits 6s yes

mod rist rank tideborne 0
wait 1
mod rist show
printed gasp: not carved yes
